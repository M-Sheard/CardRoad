# Isometric town

Same seven screens as board-and-pawns, in the 3/4 walking-town language Marc liked from `mockups/display-more/07-isometric-town.jpg`.

You are a tiny cream-coat avatar. People are other walkers. Places are buildings you walk to. The match is an isometric parlour table.

1. **Hub** — the valley as a walkable map. Lythmere, Mill, Quay, Club.
2. **Town** — Lythmere market. Tomas, Nessa, Bram, Elira standing at stalls.
3. **Challenge** — walk up, cream terms plaque, Challenge / Back.
4. **Match** — 3×3 gilt creature cards on felt, you south, Tomas north.
5. **Binder** — home desk, collection open.
6. **Saturday** — Club lawn fills with walkers.
7. **Travel** — coach on the road, Thursday.

Live `index.html` unchanged. This would be a different product shell from board-and-pawns (a walkable town instead of pieces on a printed map).
