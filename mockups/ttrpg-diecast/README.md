# Five TTRPG table settings (design ideas)

Look-test only. Live `index.html` and the oak-bevel standees are **not** changed.

Marc asked for tabletop-RPG settings we could steal from — **grid or hex**, **items sitting on the board**, people remodeled as **die-cast minis** (not plywood cutouts).

Open `index.html` to flip through them.

## What would change if we picked one

- Town display: painted isometric street → a board (square tiles, hexes, or 3D terrain) with scatter pieces.
- NPCs: oak-bevel cutouts → 28mm die-cast / painted metal figures on round bases.
- Play: tap a square/hex rather than walk a painting.

That is a design + art lock change. Do not ship it until Marc picks.

## Why these, not more parchment

The last Lythmere parchment map was still a *picture*. These are *tables*: grid/hex you can count, barrels and stalls as pieces, people as metal toys.
