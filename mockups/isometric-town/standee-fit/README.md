# Standee fit — look-test only

Marc asked to **keep the oak NPC standees** (Tomas, Nessa, Bram, Elira) on the isometric Market, and to try changing the **town’s colour / finish** so those figures sit in the street instead of on top of it.

Locked product is still tiny walking avatars (`../README.md`). This folder does not change that. Live `index.html` is untouched.

Standees in these stills are the real cutouts (`assets/npcs/tomas_standee_bevel_v19.png`, Nessa/Bram/Elira v22), dropped onto empty streets. Background only changes.

Open `index.html` to tap through them on the phone chrome.

## What clashes

The map is muted storybook paint (slate, teal river, dry gouache). The characters are glossy jewel-tone oil on oak. Finish and colour, not the camera.

Scale is the other clash: live street-standee size dwarfs these cottages. These mocks use **map-token size** so the oak plaque still reads.

## Background shifts

1. **As-is town** — control. Same Market paint. Teal river vs cobalt/emerald clothes.
2. **Oak playset** — town painted on a wooden board. Honey grain in the sky and river. Pieces look like toys.
3. **Jewel gold** — cobalt / emerald / magenta awnings, terracotta roofs, warmer river. Closest colour match to the figures.
4. **Oil finish** — glossier varnish on the same layout. Subtle; easy to miss next to as-is.
5. **Warm dusk** — lantern hour, gold sky and windows, river reflecting amber. Figures pick up the same warmth.
6. **Umber greige** — olive water, taupe cobbles, linen plaster. Quiet town; standees are the colour.
7. **Pale set** — too far. Market stalls wash out; figures look stuck on a picture book.

Four-NPC stills sit on jewel, dusk, oak, and umber.
