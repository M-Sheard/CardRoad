# Board, map, pawns

Marc locked option 6 from `mockups/display-ten/`. This folder is that language on the screens that actually get used.

People are wooden pawns. Places are printed spaces. The match is a felt inset on the same table. Nobody stands inside a painted street.

## How a week would play

1. **Hub** — the valley board. Who is where. Wednesday token. Saturday disc at Club.
2. **Town** — tap a space, it enlarges. Whoever is there stands on that oval. Colour key: yellow you, blue Tomas, green Nessa, red Bram.
3. **Challenge** — tap a pawn. A cream identity card and Accept / Back come onto the table.
4. **Match** — felt 3×3, gilt creature cards, your pawn south, theirs north. The map stays in the background.
5. **Binder** — collection is another object on the same oak, not a different world.
6. **Saturday** — the Club fills with pawns. Fixture card on the table.
7. **Travel** — your pawn walks the printed road. A day token moves with it.

## What this buys

- No 2.5D occlusion. Furniture never has to sit in front of a person.
- New towns are a new printed space, not a new street painting with pads.
- The calendar, the coach, and Saturday are the same objects as the people.
