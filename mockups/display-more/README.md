# Fifteen more ways to display Card Road

Board-and-pawns stays on standby (`mockups/board-pawns/`). These are different shells so we can check that lock before building it.

Same game: living card world, 3×3 matches, calendar, collection, several towns. Not people standing inside a painted street. Not the first ten from `mockups/display-ten/`.

1. **Coach window** — you travel inside the carriage. Towns are the view. Match on the fold-down table.
2. **Dollhouse** — cutaway rooms. People are dolls. Club, Inn, Market stacked.
3. **Visual novel** — painted backdrop, bust portraits, talk box. People are not in the painting.
4. **Book of Hours** — the week is an illuminated page. People live in the margins.
5. **Magic lantern** — towns are glass slides. People are extra slides overlaid.
6. **Flannelgraph** — felt valley, felt people. Closest cousin to board-and-pawns; different material.
7. **Isometric town** — you walk a tiny painted Lythmere as an avatar.
8. **Scrapbook** — the location is a postcard. People are carte-de-visite photos.
9. **Shop windows** — each place is a glazed diorama. Glass is the frame.
10. **JRPG battle** — overworld cut to a command-menu match with gilt creature cards.
11. **Peep-show** — look through a hole into stacked paper depth.
12. **Tapestry** — the week is embroidery. People are stitches.
13. **Snow globes** — each town is a globe on the mantel. Saturday is the one you pick up.
14. **Club room** — you never leave the smoking room. A wall map with pins is the world. Matches happen at the table in front of you.
15. **View-Master** — click a reel of towns. People live inside the stereo picture.

Live `index.html` unchanged. Look-tests only.
