Lythmere — six 2.5D viewpoints

The axis here is the camera, not the paint. All six put the Inn, Market, Club and Riverside in one 390×844 phone frame under the live paper chrome, with a tappable tag on each landmark. The first four give you a flat background plane with solid pieces standing in front of it, which is the thing an aerial map cannot do.

The earlier aerial set, for contrast

These eight were the miss. They vary the paint finish but share one bird's-eye camera, so there is no background plane for anything to stand against — the ground is the entire picture.
